Life expectancy vs. GDP per capita, 1810–2020

A rough recreation of Hans Rosling's “200 Countries, 200 Years”. Each bubble is one of 195 countries: GDP per capita left to right (log scale), life expectancy bottom to top, color by world region, size by population. Press play, hover a bubble to name it, and search or click to follow up to three.
